    FAQ

    Good to know

    Do you supply the alcohol?

    We can supply everything, or work with alcohol you buy — we'll send a shopping list so nothing goes to waste.

    How far in advance should I book?

    Weekends fill up fast — 4 to 8 weeks ahead is ideal, but message us for last-minute dates.

    Can you create a drink just for our event?

    Yes. Signature drinks are designed around your colours, theme or story, and named however you like.

    What do you need at the venue?

    A flat space about 3 × 2 m and a power point nearby. We bring the rest.
