Who we are

Premium, yet affordable.

TWS Liquid Chef creates beverage experiences for the moments that matter — corporate functions, weddings, private celebrations and children's parties.

We're not a cheap catering company, and we're not an exclusive luxury-only service. We sit deliberately in between: the presentation and precision of a fine cocktail lounge, delivered with warmth, organisation and pricing that makes sense for real events in Botswana.

Every menu is built around the people in the room — from brand-coloured cocktails for a product launch to rainbow mocktails for a fifth birthday.

Founder & Lead Mixologist

TWS stands for Tiro Wabotlhe Sebeso — the name behind every pour.

Formally trained, with three years of mixology experience, Tiro honed the craft in professional hospitality, including at Maun Wilderness Safari. Working as mixologist, chef and waiter gave a full view of what makes guests feel looked after — and that is the standard TWS is built on.

Tiro has catered multiple events and assisted at corporate functions and weddings, consistently receiving positive guest feedback.
